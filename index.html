The High Stakes endgame: lifting the robot's own weight up the ladder. The mechanism has to be light enough to carry for a whole match and strong enough to matter for the last ten seconds.

Autonomy

Line Following Robot

Fully autonomous, with obstacle avoidance and encoder + IMU sensor fusion. It also draws a heat map of everywhere it's been. Underneath: hand-wired perfboard, motor drivers and encoder-equipped gearmotors, packed into a 3D printed chassis with nothing to spare.

Manufacturing

RC Car, Designed for 20,000 Units

A glass fibre chassis, 3D printed roll cage, cast aluminium rims and a thermoformed ABS shell, then costed for a production run of 20,000: sand-cast A356 rims at $2.50 each, an injection-moulded ABS roll cage at $3.20 on a 9:50 cycle, a vacuum-thermoformed shell at $5.65, and a printed glass-fibre chassis extension at $4.42. Six-person team; I owned the roll cage, its injection mould, and the aeroshell design analysis and material selection.
