Wrist four-bar linkage simulator

Enter the axis and pin positions from your sketch. Arm lengths, rest angles and rod length are worked out from them. The top view is drawn in your coordinates; the side view shows the z offsets.

Geometry

Axes are x, y, z in one CAD frame (mm). Each pin is measured from the center of its own axis (motor pin from the motor center, wrist pin from the wrist center), along the same x, y, z directions. z is along the joint axes.
Angle °Length
Angle °Length
Pin angles are measured counter-clockwise from the +x direction of your CAD frame (0° = +x, 90° = +y). Edit x, y or angle and length; the other pair updates.

Requirements

Swings both pins around their axes (arm lengths fixed) to get the highest worst-case transmission angle across ± the required travel.
