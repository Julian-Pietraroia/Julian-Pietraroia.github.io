Printing tips

  • Material: TPU 95A grips and flexes with the board. PETG works but is stiffer.
  • Settings: 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 25–40% infill, print slowly (about 25 mm/s for TPU).
  • One color, two colors: print the pad in one color, or pause at the top of the pad layer to switch filament for the raised grip.
  • Mounting: back the pad with outdoor-rated double-sided tape (e.g. 3M VHB). Clean the topsheet with isopropyl alcohol, press hard, and let it cure for 24 hours before riding.
