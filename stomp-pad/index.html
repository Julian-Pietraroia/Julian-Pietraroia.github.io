    Printing tips

    • Material: TPU 95A grips and flexes with the board. PETG works but is stiffer.
    • Settings: 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 25–40% infill, print slowly (about 25 mm/s for TPU).
    • Colours: open the 3MF in Bambu Studio and accept the colour dialog. Each colour maps to an AMS slot. With no AMS, print in one colour, or pause at the top of the pad layer to swap filament for the raised grip.
    • Reflective tape: use a retroreflective vinyl tape (e.g. 3M Scotchlite or Oralite). Cut it to the size shown above, round the corners slightly, clean the recess with isopropyl alcohol and press it in firmly.
    • Mounting: back the pad with outdoor-rated double-sided tape (e.g. 3M VHB). Clean the topsheet with isopropyl alcohol, press hard, and let it cure for 24 hours before riding.
